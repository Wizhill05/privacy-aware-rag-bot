# 2026 Engineering Salary Bands & Executive Compensation

CONFIDENTIAL - MANAGERS AND PEOPLE OPS ONLY.
Do not distribute outside executive management and line managers.

## Compensation Tiers (Base Salary in USD)
- **Software Engineer I (L1)**: $110,000 - $135,000 per annum + 5% target bonus.
- **Software Engineer II (L2)**: $140,000 - $175,000 per annum + 10% target bonus.
- **Senior Software Engineer (L3)**: $180,000 - $225,000 per annum + 15% target bonus.
- **Staff Software Engineer (L4)**: $230,000 - $290,000 per annum + 20% target bonus.
- **Principal Software Engineer (L5)**: $300,000 - $380,000 per annum + 25% target bonus.
- **Engineering Manager (M1)**: $210,000 - $260,000 per annum + 20% target bonus.
- **Director of Engineering (M2)**: $280,000 - $360,000 per annum + 30% target bonus.

## Equity Grants & Merit Adjustments
Equity refreshers take place every September. Managers may authorize discretionary retention bonuses up to $25,000 with VP Engineering sign-off.
Salary adjustments for cost of living are capped at 4.5% for fiscal year 2026.
