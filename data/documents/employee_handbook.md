# Acme Global Employee Handbook & Workplace Policy

Welcome to Acme Global! This handbook outlines company guidelines and benefits applicable to all active full-time and part-time staff.

## Working Hours and Remote Work Policy
- Standard working hours: 40 hours per week with core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time.
- Flexible Remote Work: Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week, or 100% remote if designated as remote-first upon hiring.
- Equipment allowance: Every new joiner receives a $1,500 home office ergonomics setup stipend.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves
- **Vacation**: 25 business days of paid annual vacation leave.
- **Sick Leave**: 12 days per calendar year.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers.
- **Volunteering**: 2 paid civic/volunteer days each year.

## Health, Wellness & Perks
- Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage starting day one.
- Monthly $150 health and gym subsidy.
- Annual learning budget of $1,200 per employee for books, conferences, and certifications.
